Profile

Changing the currency converts all your saved amounts (expenses, budget, savings goals and recurring bills) at today's exchange rate. This needs an internet connection.

Categories

Add your own categories, rename them or change their colour. The built-in ones stay as they are. Deleting a category moves its expenses to Other.

Storage on this device

Keep an encrypted copy of your data in a folder on your device, outside the browser. Choose a folder once and Expensiz updates the file after every change.

Danger zone

Deletes all your Expensiz data from this device, your device folder and your cloud backup. Your account stays so you can start fresh. This cannot be undone.

Notifications

Real notifications on this device (Windows or Android), not just messages inside the app. Choose what you want to hear about.

Cloud backup (MEGA)

Your data is encrypted on this device with a key made from your Expensiz password before anything is stored or uploaded. MEGA only holds scrambled data. If you forget your Expensiz password, a backup cannot be recovered.
